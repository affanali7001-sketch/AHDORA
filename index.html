Everything in one app

No separate namaz app, Quran app and study app. AHDORA connects your Deen and your daily routine, so progress in one place shows up everywhere.

Salah

Accurate prayer times for your city and calculation method, with azan, start and deadline reminders. Mark each prayer Prayed, Missed or Qaza — honestly, never assumed.

Quran Book

Set your sleep and wake times, get wind-down reminders, and let AHDORA warn you five minutes before it locks your chosen apps for the night.

Common questions

Is AHDORA free?

Yes. AHDORA is free to download and use — Salah, Quran Book, study planner, focus, challenges and the Hijri calendar are all included. There are no charges for installing from the APK or from F-Droid.

How do I install the APK?

Tap Download APK, open the downloaded file, and allow installation when Android asks. Then open AHDORA, create your account and complete the one-time setup — it asks about your schedule, prayer calculation method and sleep times so the app can set itself up for you.

What is F-Droid, and why is AHDORA there?

F-Droid is a free, open-source app store for Android. For now, add AHDORA's repo address — shown in the Download section above — inside the F-Droid app and install AHDORA 1.0.1 from there; updates then come to you automatically. AHDORA's official F-Droid listing, where you could just search the name, is coming soon. And if your phone does not have F-Droid yet, the direct APK download on this page works without any store.

Why does AHDORA ask for permissions?

Every permission has a specific job — notifications for azan and reminders, app-usage access for the prayer-time and sleep-time app lock that you switch on yourself, and so on. Granting a permission does not steal your data; your records stay tied to your own account. The tutorial video at the top of this page goes through every permission one by one, and also shows how to grant a permission again — including allowing Restricted settings — if you declined it earlier.

Is my data private?

Yes. Your Salah records, study data and profile are tied to your account only — no other user can see them. Friends can only see the challenge progress you choose to share together: the challenge name, day, completion and points. Passwords are stored hashed, never in plain text.

Which prayer calculation methods are supported?

During setup you choose your country, city, timezone and calculation method, and whether Asr follows the Shafi'i or Hanafi school. You can adjust individual prayer times later from your profile if your local masjid follows slightly different timings.

Can AHDORA really block apps at prayer and sleep time?

Yes — on Android, with permissions you grant yourself. At prayer time your chosen apps are covered until you confirm you have prayed; at sleep time you get a five-minute warning before the lock. Calls and emergency access always stay available, and you control which apps are included. It is optional — turn it on only if you want it.

I found a problem. How do I get help?

Write to us at support@ahdora.me and tell us what happened — which screen you were on and what you expected. Every report is read.
