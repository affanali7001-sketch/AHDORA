AHDORA is an Islamic lifestyle and productivity app that brings Salah, Quran, study, focus, sleep and challenges together in one place.

Salah: live prayer times by city, prayer tracker with Qaza support, monthly calendar and reminders.

Quran: the full Quran as a real mushaf book (604 pages, Uthmani script), continuous tilawat audio with Urdu tarjuma, and a daily reading log.

Study: subjects, tasks, daily planner and a real Pomodoro timer.

Focus and sleep: focus sessions, sleep schedule and reminders.

Challenges: 30-day and 365-day challenges with friends, points and badges.

Friends: connect with friends only when both sides accept — never automatic.

Hijri calendar: Hijri and Gregorian dates, Ramadan, Eid and other big days with countdowns, plus a mosque finder.

Every action is saved to your own account and works across devices. No fake data, no fake statistics — only your real activity.
